Project information

Qayyim

Qayyim is an AI-powered automated exam grading system designed to streamline the assessment process for computer science courses in Egyptian universities with 90% accuracy.
1. Instructor Portal: Instructors upload exam papers and model answers, configure grading rubrics, and review AI-generated assessments with human-in-the-loop oversight for quality assurance.
2. Automated Grading Engine: The system processes handwritten responses using OCR extraction, evaluates answers against reference materials through RAG-based AI analysis, and generates detailed feedback with grade-aware criticism levels.
3. Student Feedback System: Students receive comprehensive feedback on their submissions with precise scoring on a 0-1 scale (0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1.0), helping them understand their performance and areas for improvement.
4. Privacy-First Architecture: Deployed on university servers with secure authentication and local data processing to protect sensitive academic information.
